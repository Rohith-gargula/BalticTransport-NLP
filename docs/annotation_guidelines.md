# Annotation guidelines
Assign exactly one primary topic.

- **delay**: late arrival/departure, disruption to schedule, delivery delay, congestion causing delay.
- **safety**: accidents, injury, hazardous conditions, safety rules or risk prevention.
- **tariffs**: fares, freight rates, tolls, pricing, tariff changes or policy.
- **infrastructure**: roads, railways, ports, terminals, construction, maintenance, capacity.
- **other**: transport-related text that does not primarily match the above.

## Rules
1. Label the main event, not every secondary mention.
2. If two labels are equally central, note ambiguity and adjudicate with a second reviewer.
3. Do not infer facts absent from the text.
4. Preserve source language; do not machine-translate for primary labeling.
5. Double-annotate a random subset (target: 100 rows) and calculate Cohen's kappa.
6. Record annotator, date, and adjudication in a separate annotation log; do not expose personal information.
